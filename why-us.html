Integrity First

Hardware Transparency Over Hype

The portable flash storage market often obscures critical technical variables behind marketing catchphrases. We take the opposite approach.

We openly publish full specification tables for every USB drive model in our catalog. Clients know the controller brand, USB interface generation, sequential read and write speeds, and NAND flash tier before ordering.

By testing bulk shipments using industry-standard diagnostic tools such as H2testw, we ensure that every gigabyte declared is genuine, preventing the counterfeit capacities and corrupted data risks common in unverified commodity flash drives.
