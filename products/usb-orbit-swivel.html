Orbit Swivel Flash Drive

MODEL NUMBER: USB-S300

The Orbit Swivel USB-S300 is the world's most trusted commercial flash drive design. Featuring a protective 360-degree rotational aluminum clip mounted over a soft-touch matte composite body, it eliminates lost caps while providing a generous, flat surface for corporate logo imprinting and laser engraving.

Verified Engineering Highlights

  • Capless 360-degree rotating aluminum clip prevents connector damage without caps to lose
  • Dual-sided branding area accommodates multi-color silkscreen and fiber laser marking
  • Available in both cost-effective USB 2.0 and high-speed USB 3.0 controller configurations
  • Ergonomic lanyard loop at the tail of the metal clip for neck straps and keychains
  • Vibrant color combinations: mix-and-match colored clips and composite bodies
