SecureVault Key Flash Drive

MODEL NUMBER: USB-K500

The SecureVault Key USB-K500 is crafted entirely from precision-stamped 304 stainless steel in the exact shape of a standard door key. Utilizing monolithic Chip-On-Board (UDP) packaging, the flash memory controller and NAND cells are permanently encapsulated against moisture, deep immersion, drops, and particulate ingress.

Verified Engineering Highlights

  • IPX7 certified waterproof rating: survives accidental submersion up to 1 meter depth
  • Monolithic 304 stainless-steel construction with high tensile strength
  • Large keyhead loop slides naturally onto standard automotive and household keyrings
  • Exposed gold-plated direct tongue connector with zero moving parts and no cap to lose
  • Scratch-resistant fiber laser engraving surface for permanent corporate identification
