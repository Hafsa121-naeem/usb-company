DuoLink Pro Dual OTG Drive

MODEL NUMBER: USB-D200

The DuoLink Pro USB-D200 bridges traditional USB-A workstations and modern USB Type-C laptops, tablets, and smartphones. Featuring both reversible Type-C and standard Type-A plugs enclosed within a 360-degree rotating brushed aluminum protective shield, it eliminates the need for cumbersome dongles and adapters.

Verified Engineering Highlights

  • Reversible USB Type-C connector compatible with Android, iPadOS, macOS, and Windows
  • High-bandwidth USB 3.2 Gen 1 controller providing sequential reads up to 150 MB/s
  • Capless design with 360-degree aluminum protective rotating bridge and knurled grip
  • Direct On-The-Go (OTG) instant file backup without cloud connectivity or cellular data
  • Cobalt blue anodized aluminum core with laser-engraved serial and capacity labels
