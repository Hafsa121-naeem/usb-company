CardSlim Executive Drive

MODEL NUMBER: USB-C400

The CardSlim Executive USB-C400 is an ultra-slim 2.2mm wafer flash drive designed precisely to standard credit card dimensions (85.5 mm × 54 mm). Featuring an innovative flip-out gold-plated Chip-On-Board (UDP) connector, it slips into any wallet credit card slot while providing maximum surface area for high-resolution edge-to-edge branding.

Verified Engineering Highlights

  • Dimensions match standard ISO 7810 ID-1 credit cards for effortless wallet storage
  • Dual-sided, full-bleed digital UV photographic printing capabilities (up to 1200 DPI)
  • Integrated flip-out gold-plated UDP flash memory controller module
  • Waterproof, dustproof, and shock-resistant internal COB chip architecture
  • High utility for digital business cards, product catalogs, and corporate media press kits
