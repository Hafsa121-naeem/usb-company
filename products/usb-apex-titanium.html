Apex Titanium Flash Drive

MODEL NUMBER: USB-M100

The Apex Titanium USB-M100 is an ultra-durable flash drive engineered from die-cast zinc alloy. Its monolithic seamless body shields internal NAND components against physical shock, static pressure, dust, and daily wear, making it an ideal choice for corporate personnel, technicians, and everyday keyring carry.

Verified Engineering Highlights

  • High-durability zinc-alloy construction with natural thermal dissipation properties
  • High-speed USB 3.2 Gen 1 standard transfer rates (backward-compatible with USB 2.0)
  • Integrated ergonomic lanyard/keychain aperture for secure daily portability
  • Precision laser engraving surface for permanent corporate asset tagging and logos
  • Amber activity indicator LED for read/write operation monitoring
